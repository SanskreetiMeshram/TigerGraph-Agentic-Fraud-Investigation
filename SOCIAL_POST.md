## 1. LinkedIn Post

🚀 Excited to unveil **CaseGuard: Uncertainty-Gated Agentic Fraud Investigation**, built for the **TigerGraph × Hacker House Goa 2026 Hackathon**!

Financial fraud teams are overwhelmed by slow, fragmented investigations—often identifying fraud only after the money is gone. Most automated tools force a binary "fraud vs. not fraud" decision on isolated risk scores, either over-blocking innocent customers or missing coordinated syndicates.

We took a radically different approach: **an AI investigator that knows what it doesn't know.**

Key Highlights:
🔹 **TigerGraph & GSQL Backbone**: Executes sub-millisecond graph traversals (`card_window`, `device_neighbors`, `region_burst`) across 590,000+ IEEE-CIS transactions to detect hidden device sharing, velocity bursts, and out-of-region anomalies.
🔹 **Uncertainty-Gating**: Explicitly calculates confidence vs. risk. If signals are ambiguous (<0.70), it triggers policy-approved controlled evidence gathering rather than guessing.
🔹 **Dynamic Next-Best Actions (NBA)**: Formulates defensible actions before and after evidence arrives, with strict institutional approval routing (`auto`, `L1`, `L2`).
🔹 **Graph Memory Persistence**: Writes closed cases back to TigerGraph as persistent case memory—enabling future investigations to instantly recall past travel destinations and fraud patterns!
🔹 **100% Benchmark Accuracy**: Evaluated across all 20 official benchmark cases with zero validation errors.
🔹 **Modern Light-Theme Analyst Cockpit**: Designed with a clean, light palette featuring interactive canvas graph visualizers and turn-by-turn investigation timelines.

Huge thanks to @TigerGraphDB and the Hacker House Goa mentors for hosting an incredible challenge!

## 2. X (Twitter) Post / Thread

🚨 Introducing **CaseGuard**: Autonomous Agentic Fraud Investigation powered by @TigerGraphDB! 🕵️‍♂️⚡

Traditional fraud models rely on binary scores and guess blindly on edge cases. CaseGuard is an AI investigator that **knows what it doesn't know**. 🧵👇

1/6 🔍 **Graph Analytics with GSQL**: Instead of asking LLMs to do math, CaseGuard executes native GSQL queries over 590K+ IEEE-CIS transactions to detect micro-testing velocity, device syndicates, and out-of-region card-present anomalies in milliseconds.

2/6 🛡️ **Uncertainty-Gated Reasoning**: When confidence is insufficient, CaseGuard doesn't guess. It triggers policy-governed controlled evidence requests (cardholder push pings, 2FA step-up) before executing any block.

3/6 🔄 **Next-Best Action (NBA) Evolution**: Recommends defensible actions both BEFORE and AFTER evidence arrives, strictly governed by institutional approval routes (`auto`, `L1`, `L2`).

4/6 🧠 **TigerGraph Case Memory**: Every closed case is persisted back into the graph. When Case HHG-012 flagged an out-of-region charge, graph memory recalled prior Case CC-0003 confirming cardholder travel, clearing the false alarm automatically!

5/6 📄 **FinCEN SAR Generation**: Autonomously drafts complete 6–12 sentence regulatory narratives when thresholds are met ($1,000+ or coordinated rings).

6/6 🎨 Features a sleek, modern light-theme Analyst Dashboard with interactive canvas graph visualizers. Evaluated across all 20 benchmark cases with 100% compliance!
